section: Speakers
---
- Nook can name the people in a Zoom call. With *Name speakers from Zoom* on in Settings, it reads participant names and Zoom's active speaker while it records, and puts a name to a voice when the match is clear. This works with the Zoom desktop app only, not Zoom in a web browser. Naming a voice by hand now offers the people from the call, or the calendar invitees, with one click.
