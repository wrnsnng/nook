# Nook 1.6.3

This update makes automatic meeting detection substantially more reliable.

- Meetings now end cleanly when Teams, Zoom, Webex, or FaceTime leaves an old
  meeting window onscreen after the call.
- Google Meet detection now understands Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Brave,
  Arc, Opera, and Vivaldi.
- Native meeting apps with ambiguous window titles are verified against their
  local audio activity, reducing false prompts.
- Dismissed or completed meeting prompts stay dismissed until a genuine new
  call begins.

Everything remains processed and stored locally on your Mac.
