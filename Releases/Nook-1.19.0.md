# Nook 1.19.0

Your words reach the note first, and the controls around them are calmer and
safer.

- Nook now saves the transcript before it starts summary work. A summary that
  is cancelled, times out, or fails cannot take the original conversation with
  it. Recording into an existing note follows the same transcript-first rule.
- Editing a note title is deliberate: click the pencil, type, press Return to
  save, or press Escape to cancel. Empty titles and conflicting filenames are
  rejected, and a rename waits until your Markdown edits are safely written.
- Summaries are broken into readable paragraphs and grounded with the My notes
  and flagged moments that deserve attention. Template checklist examples no
  longer leak into Open Actions, and repeated speaker labels no longer crowd a
  run of transcript lines.
- A spoken note now looks like a spoken note. It shows Spoken words and the
  sections that belong to it, without empty meeting-only areas.
- Settings is easier to navigate. Keyboard shortcuts, dictation behavior,
  app-specific overrides, permissions, and note actions have clear groups and
  descriptions. Shortcut recording stays in place, reports conflicts, and can
  capture modifier-only combinations.
- Listening settings now includes an audio input check with separate You and
  Meeting meters. The check stays on this Mac, records nothing, transcribes
  nothing, and leaves no file behind.
- Interrupted recordings are visible in the library with Recover, Reveal, and
  Delete actions, plus their date, size, and recovery state. Failures remain
  visible instead of pretending cleanup succeeded.
- Quick Note and dictation no longer accept late results from an earlier edit,
  closed window, cancelled session, or replaced request. Deleting a note always
  uses the Trash, and the note stays put if the Trash is unavailable.

Everything remains local-first. The audio check and summary improvements do not
send recordings, transcripts, notes, or model output off this Mac.
