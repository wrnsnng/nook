# Nook 1.23.0

Nook helps file your meetings, and several screens are clearer.

**Folders that fill themselves**

- Nook suggests a folder for a note that is not in one yet, under its title,
  with the reason: earlier meetings in the same series are there, the folder's
  name is in the title or is a named speaker, or the note's title matches the
  notes in that folder. Move it with one click or dismiss the suggestion.
- A new recording of a recurring meeting is filed with its earlier meetings
  automatically, with a notice and Undo. A note you move yourself stays where
  you put it. Turn this off in Settings, General, "File recurring meetings
  automatically".
- A note's Move To menu lists the suggested folder first.

**Clearer screens**

- The prep brief reads like a note, with Record This Meeting as the main
  action, action items that show who owns them and open the note they came
  from, and a list of earlier meetings.
- Ask Your Library shows your question as a heading, sets the answer apart,
  and lists the notes it used as rows you can click.
- Interrupted recordings and recovered drafts in the sidebar read like note
  rows, with one clear action each.
- A fallback summary explains itself once, inside the write-up, with Retry.
- When recording stops because a permission is missing, the notch offers Open
  Settings.
- Progress while a meeting is saved fits the notch, setup keeps its titles in
  place between steps, and About uses Nook's accent for its links.

Nook remains local-first. Folder suggestions are worked out on this Mac from
your own titles and folders. This release adds no telemetry, network service
or remote model.
