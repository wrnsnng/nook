# Nook 1.16.0

Long meetings finally get the write-up they deserve.

- Summaries of long conversations now carry their specifics: figures,
  names, product words, and decisions come through in close-to-spoken
  wording instead of dissolving into general themes on the way to the
  final note.
- Key commitments are harvested directly from the raw transcript before
  any condensing happens, and reach the write-up as the most reliable
  record of what was said. A two hour meeting no longer has to survive
  five rewrites for its follow-ups to land.
- The note is written with the meeting's scale in mind: a two hour
  brainstorm reads differently from a fifteen minute check-in, and
  longer meetings may now write proportionally longer summaries.

Everything stays on this Mac, and every item is still checked against
your transcript before it reaches a note.
