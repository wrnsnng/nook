# Nook 1.5

The meeting workspace now feels like it belongs to the MacBook display itself.

## Refined

- A rebuilt edge-black meeting surface that grows naturally from the camera area.
- The panel now anchors to the display’s literal top edge instead of the usable area beneath the menu bar.
- The panel stays above the menu bar while open, completing the physical-bezel illusion.
- Cleaner live controls and a calmer, more compact meeting-detection prompt.
- More natural centring and motion as the workspace changes size.

## Still private by design

- Recording, transcription, summaries, and Markdown notes remain local to your Mac.
- The update is Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and verified by Sparkle before installation.
