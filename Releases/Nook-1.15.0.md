# Nook 1.15.0

Your shortcuts, your way. And a fix for the meetings that were too long to
summarize.

- Every keyboard shortcut Nook owns can now be rebound in Settings,
  Keyboard: Flag This Moment, Start, Pause or Resume, and Finish Meeting,
  Search Commands, New Note, Save Notes, and the quick note pad's
  checklist line and discard. Click a shortcut, press the combination you
  want, and it takes effect everywhere at once, including the system-wide
  hotkey while a meeting records.
- If two actions end up on the same combination, Settings says so instead
  of letting one silently swallow the other. Each action can be reset to
  its default individually, or all at once.
- Fixed: meetings around an hour or longer reported "too long for the
  on-device model" even though they could be summarized. The condensing
  passes stalled on themselves instead of shrinking; long meetings now
  condense all the way down, and Regenerate summary works on notes that
  saved with only transcript highlights.

The macOS conventions stay as they are: Quit, Settings, Return and Escape
answers, and Cmd-W still mean what every other Mac app taught you.
