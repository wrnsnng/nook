# Nook 1.12.0

The quick note grows up: it is now a place to speak freely, and the
words it catches can carry real tasks.

- Hands-free capture: turn on "Hands-free" in the quick note and Nook
  keeps listening between thoughts until you toggle it off, press the
  dictation shortcut, or close the pad.
- The pad shows what Nook is hearing right now, live, while only settled
  words enter your note.
- File spoken words into a meeting: the pad's new "File into" menu
  appends them to a meeting's personal notes instead of saving a second
  note.
- Checklist lines in quick notes are real action items now. Anything you
  write as "- [ ] task" appears in the sidebar, takes due dates, and
  exports to Reminders like items from meetings do.
- When your words include a date, Nook offers one tap to make that line
  a dated task. It works only from words you actually said.
- New notes can start from three templates: 1:1, Standup, and Interview,
  each seeded with an ordinary checklist.
- Press ⌘K in the library to jump to any note, start recording, ask
  your library, compile the weekly digest, or open a folder, with full
  keyboard navigation.
- A Today scope in the library sidebar shows just today's meetings and
  notes.
- Action items tick from inside the note itself now, through the same
  single-line edit the sidebar uses, so every view agrees.
