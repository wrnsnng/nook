# Nook 1.6

This release makes an active meeting calmer, clearer, and easier to control.

- The menu-bar recording state now includes a stable timer and no longer jumps while its menu is open.
- The top panel can expand, collapse, or disappear completely, with controls arranged around the MacBook camera housing.
- Processing can be cancelled to discard an accidental recording.
- Meeting titles now use the actual subject of the conversation whenever one can be identified.
- The live workspace now uses the clearer “Summary” label.
- Notes placeholders and insertion points align consistently in the top panel, floating window, and meeting library.
- The cobalt Nook icon is now applied explicitly to the Dock and app switcher after an update.
