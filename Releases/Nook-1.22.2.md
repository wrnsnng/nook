# Nook 1.22.2

Work with several notes at once, and make folders from where folders are.

- Select several notes in the library: Shift-click or Shift-arrow for a range,
  Command-click to add or remove one, Command-A for every note shown. With two
  or more selected, move them to a folder or to the Trash together, or merge
  two of them. Right-clicking or dragging a selected note acts on the whole
  selection, as in Finder.
- The Folders list ends with a New Folder row.
- The All, Today and Yesterday control keeps its size when a notice appears.

Nook remains local-first. This release adds no telemetry, network service or
remote model.
