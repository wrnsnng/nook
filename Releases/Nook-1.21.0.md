# Nook 1.21.0

A new Nook that looks and feels like it came with your Mac, a notch that
comes alive during meetings, and notes that know who said what.

**The notch**

- The notch is now a single island that grows out of the camera and folds
  back into it when a meeting is saved. While you record it sits either side
  of the camera, a waveform on one side and the clock on the other, and
  covers nothing below the menu bar. Hover to bring down the controls.
- A soft light along its edge follows the voice, sweeps while your notes are
  being written, and flashes when they are saved.
- Take a note without leaving your call: press Option-Command-N, or the
  pencil on the notch, type a line and press Return. Each line joins My notes.
- Point at the notch when nothing is happening to record, write a quick note
  or open the library. With calendar context on, it also appears shortly
  before a meeting starts. You can turn this off in Settings, General.
- Meeting prompts show the meeting app's own icon, flagged moments show in
  live captions, and long meetings show real progress while notes are
  written.

**Who said what**

- After a meeting, Nook tells the other voices apart on this Mac and labels
  them Speaker 1, Speaker 2. Name them once from the transcript and every line
  they said, the summary, search and Ask use the name. Your own voice stays
  You. Turn it off in Settings, Listening.

**After the meeting**

- Draft Follow-up writes a recap for email or chat from the note: the gist,
  decisions, next steps with owners and dates, and open questions. Review it,
  copy it or open it in Mail. Nook never sends anything itself.
- Share a note's summary or its Markdown file from the toolbar.
- Action items show their owner when the item names one, such as
  "Maya: refine the copy".
- Review the transcript behind any summary line, correct or remove it, and
  undo. Summaries keep open questions, and you can choose a Standup,
  One-to-one or Interview emphasis before regenerating.
- Saved meetings write their words first and finish the summary in the
  background, with Cancel and Retry, so nothing waits on the summary.

**Everywhere on your Mac**

- Shortcuts and Siri can start, pause, flag and finish a recording, take a
  note, ask your library a question, and open a meeting.
- Meetings appear in Spotlight by title, summary, key points and decisions.
  Turn it off in Settings, General.

**Also new**

- A calmer, native design throughout: standard macOS controls, a toolbar for
  each note, one readable column, and a new lagoon teal accent and app icon.
- Setup shows what each step is for on a small animated Mac.
- Command-palette search finds typos and words inside transcripts, and the
  library has a Yesterday filter.
- Quick Note can file a note into an existing meeting when you are done, and
  understands "scratch that" as a correction you review before it applies.
- Microphone and meeting audio are kept as separate sources through capture,
  export and recovery, and the audio check in Settings stops cleanly and
  links straight to the right permission.

Nook remains local-first. Audio, transcripts, summaries and notes stay on this
Mac unless you explicitly consent to the existing optional CLI note-action
provider. Speaker separation runs on this Mac with models included in the app,
and Spotlight's index stays on this Mac. This release adds no telemetry or
remote model service.
