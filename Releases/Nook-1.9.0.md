# Nook 1.9.0

Nook now helps before, during, and after the meeting: it can read your
calendar for context, mark moments while recording, and turn action items
into follow-through.

- Optional calendar awareness names a detected meeting after its event
  instead of a window title, and posts one quiet notification shortly before
  an event starts. Off by default; calendar access is requested only when you
  switch it on, and recording still always asks first.
- Flag a moment while recording with Option-Command-F, the panel button, or
  the menu-bar command. Flagged moments appear in the note as jumps into the
  transcript.
- When a meeting's audio is kept, every transcript line has a play button and
  a small transport follows playback through the conversation.
- Unfinished action items from all your notes are collected under "Open
  actions" in the library sidebar. Tick one off to update exactly that line
  in its file, or send an item to Reminders from its context menu.
- Reloading the library is faster: unchanged files are no longer re-read and
  re-decoded on every activation.
