# Nook 1.7.1

Dictation now works in the apps where most people actually type.

- Dictation into Chrome, Safari, Claude, ChatGPT, Obsidian, Proton Mail, and
  any text field on a web page. These describe their contents to macOS only
  when something asks, so Nook found no text field and opened a note instead.
  It now asks first.
- Nook waits until you stop speaking before deciding where your words belong.
  A note no longer appears the moment you start talking, and a field that takes
  a second to become available still receives what you said.
- A quick note left open in the corner no longer captures dictation meant for
  another app. Words go to the note only while you are working in it.
- Dictation says when it heard nothing at all, rather than closing in silence,
  so a muted microphone looks like a muted microphone.
