# Nook 1.11.0

One conversation can now span several sittings, and Nook helps you walk
into the next one prepared.

- Record into an existing note or merge two saved notes. Both join onto
  one continuous transcript with visible gap markers between sittings,
  kept audio joins so moment playback still works, and the summary and
  title are regenerated over the combined material. Your personal notes
  are never rewritten.
- Prep briefs: when an opted-in calendar event approaches and you have
  met that series before, Nook assembles a brief from your own notes:
  last decisions, key points, every action mentioned, and the past
  sittings. It appears as a quiet card in the library and as an action
  on the pre-meeting notification.
- Action items carry due dates. Set common dates from a quick menu, see
  overdue items flagged in a sidebar sorted soonest first, and export to
  Reminders with the date included.
- A spoken quick note can be promoted into a meeting note, keeping what
  you said as personal notes.
- The library can move a note to the Trash itself.
- Weekly digests state real conversation time across all of a note's
  sittings.
- Keyboard focus is now visible across Nook's own controls, and the
  recording consent prompt highlights what Return will do before you
  press it.
- Meeting titles save when you click away instead of vanishing when you
  navigate without pressing Return.
- Weekly digest refuses an empty week rather than saving a note full of
  zeros, and failures read as warnings instead of confirmations.
- The compact recording rail grew slightly for larger controls, every
  surface agrees on the elapsed clock past one hour, expanded recording
  animates one thing at a time, and VoiceOver can tick off and export
  action items straight from the sidebar.
