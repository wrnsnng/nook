# Nook 1.10.0

Your meeting notes can now answer questions, and dictation adapts to the
app you are in.

- Ask your library: ask anything your meetings covered and get an answer
  drawn only from your own notes, citing the meetings it used. Searching
  runs entirely on this Mac. When nothing matches closely enough, Nook says
  so instead of guessing.
- Create weekly digest compiles the last seven days into one note: real
  counts, every decision deduplicated across meetings, highlights labelled
  by their meeting, and flagged-moment totals.
- Dictate formatting commands: saying "new paragraph" or "new line" types a
  real break. Ordinary sentences that contain those words pass through
  untouched.
- Per-app dictation styles in Settings: give Mail its own habit without
  changing what happens everywhere else.
- Optional audio retention: kept meeting audio older than a chosen window is
  moved to the Trash on launch. Off by default.
- Shortcuts gains finish-recording, pause/resume, and latest-note-text
  actions for building your own automations.
